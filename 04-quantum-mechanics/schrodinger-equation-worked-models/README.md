# Solving the Schrödinger equation: thirteen worked demonstrations

This is the substantial worked-solutions volume in the quantum-mechanics collection. It develops a repeatable six-stage method—write the potential, write the equation, choose admissible solutions, apply boundary conditions, normalise or compare fluxes, and interpret the result—across thirteen standard undergraduate solution patterns.

## Demonstrations

1. One-dimensional infinite well
2. Potential step with \(E>V_0\)
3. Potential step with \(E<V_0\)
4. Finite barrier with \(E<V_0\): tunnelling
5. Finite barrier with \(E>V_0\): resonant transmission
6. Semi-infinite finite well
7. One-dimensional harmonic oscillator by power series
8. Harmonic oscillator by ladder operators
9. Three-dimensional rectangular box
10. Three-dimensional harmonic oscillator
11. General central-potential separation
12. Spherical infinite well
13. Hydrogen-like atom

The volume also contains a spherical-coordinate toolkit, derivations of the polar, cylindrical, and spherical Laplacians, a guide to separation of variables and eigenbases, revision maps, and master solution tables.
