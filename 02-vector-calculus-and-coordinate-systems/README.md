# Vector calculus and coordinate systems

This category connects local derivatives to global integrals and explains how vector formulas change when the coordinate basis itself moves.

## Subtopics

- [Integral theorems](integral-theorems/README.md) — line integrals, flux, circulation, Green’s theorem, the divergence theorem, and Stokes’ theorem.
- [Polar-coordinate vectors](polar-coordinate-vectors/README.md) — rotating basis vectors, velocity, acceleration, and kinetic energy in two dimensions.
- [Spherical Laplacian](spherical-laplacian/README.md) — scale factors, gradient, divergence, and the scalar Laplacian in spherical coordinates.

For a field-calculus route, read integral theorems before the spherical Laplacian. For a mechanics route, begin with polar-coordinate vectors.
